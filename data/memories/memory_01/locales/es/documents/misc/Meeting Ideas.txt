MEETING IDEAS
=============

FUTURE MEETING TOPICS
=====================

This document contains informal suggestions collected before future management meetings.

• Review archive improvements.
• Discuss documentation standards.
• Improve version control.
• Clarify department responsibilities.

DISCUSSION NOTES
================

A recurring suggestion was creating a single source of truth for organisational documents while still preserving historical versions.

Several participants highlighted that previous decisions were difficult to understand when older documentation was unavailable.

FUTURE CONSIDERATIONS
=====================

• Improve archive search.
• Document approval decisions.
• Maintain revision history.
